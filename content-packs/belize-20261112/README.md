# Belize completion pack — 2026-11-12

23 original Japanese four-choice questions: lifeCulture 4, japanConnection 9,
thinkConnect 10. Elementary/junior-high/high-school-standard levels share the
same questions; no unprepared English Challenge version is advertised.

The production public catalog was read before authoring: lifeCulture 6,
japanConnection 1, thinkConnect 0. The existing prompts concerned moving
houses, a soup, horses maintaining a sports field, school staffing, bus fares,
swimming safety and a volunteer's usual work at Japan Day. New questions use
different prompts and facts. Combined, each of the three categories has ten
questions; the other three categories use the existing core adventures.

Factual questions and explanations were checked against the URLs in each
question on 2026-11-12. Sources are the Belize Tourism Board's Travel Belize,
Japan's Ministry of Foreign Affairs, Embassy of Japan in Belize and JICA.
Diplomatic relations use MOFA's November 1982 date; the JICA representative
greeting's conflicting 1986 wording was not used. Past event/project questions
identify their year or period and do not claim those conditions remain current.

ThinkConnect questions are original hypothetical classroom judgments inspired
by the cited context. They are not represented as factual quotations or claims
that the described conversation occurred. No third-party photographs are copied.

`questions_all.json` is the complete source. `build-pack.py` regenerates the
five import ZIPs (5+5+5+5+3), each with a valid Mission Factory v1 manifest.
Every batch passed the repository's `validateFactoryManifest` check.
