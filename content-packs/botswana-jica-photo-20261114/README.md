# Botswana — ten JICA World Diary photo drafts

Ten original Japanese photoText4 items: lifeCulture 5, japanConnection 2,
thinkConnect 3. Each uses one of TEN DISTINCT actual single photographs from
five JICA World Diary articles. No AI images or source collages are used.

Topics: donkey cart, shared minibus, seswaa preparation, mokoro propulsion,
selecting takeaway meals, Japanese writing on reused vehicles, localization of
Japanese navigation equipment, limits of a single meal example, seasonal evidence
and the relationship between local waterways and transport.

Every question includes an answer, three distinct distractors, explanation,
observation/inference distinction, discussion prompt, author attribution, article
URL, article display date and original photo URL. Display dates are not asserted
to be photo capture dates. Volatile transport prices/routes/capacities are not
used as answer facts. No vehicle repair or alarm-reset instructions are provided.

Learning metadata selects elementary/juniorHigh/highSchoolStandard; these are
one Japanese version, not three separately rewritten versions. Photo rights
review remains pending before publication. Attribution does not constitute
permission. Existing questions are not replaced.

Run build-pack.py to generate two five-question ZIPs. Source URLs and hashes
are preserved in photo-sources.json; original files can be downloaded again.
The full article/photo sources were read and all selected photos visually checked.
Both manifests pass the existing Mission Factory schema. Each archive was checked
for matching photo bytes, valid image decoding and one photo slot per question.
